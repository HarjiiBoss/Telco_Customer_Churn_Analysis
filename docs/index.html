Executive Dashboards

Four Dashboards, One Business Story

01 — Executive Summary: churn, revenue at risk, and top-line risk indicators in one view

03 — Revenue: revenue at risk, customer value, and estimated lifetime value by segment
04 — Risk: contract dependency, service concentration, and high-risk customer profiling
Customer Pillar

Who Is Churning, and Why

Contract Type
Month-to-Month Contracts Drive Churn — 15× Higher Than Two-Year
Month-to-month customers churn at 42.71%, compared to 11.3% for one-year and just 2.8% for two-year contracts. Contract flexibility is the single strongest churn signal in the dataset.
01
Tenure
Nearly Half of Churn Happens in the First 12 Months
Customers with 0–12 months tenure churn at 47.44%, dropping to 25.5% at 13–36 months and 11.9% beyond 37 months. Early onboarding is the highest-leverage retention window.
02
Internet Service
Fiber Optic Customers Churn at 41.89% Despite Driving Most Revenue
Fiber optic churn (41.89%) is more than double DSL (19.0%) and far above no-internet customers (7.4%) — a service-specific retention challenge tied to the business's largest revenue source.
03
Payment Method
Electronic Check Is the Highest-Risk Payment Method
Electronic check customers churn at 45.29% — more than 3× the churn rate of automatic payment methods (bank transfer 16.7%, credit card 15.2%), making payment type an early behavioural churn signal.
04
Revenue Pillar

What Financial Value Is Being Lost

Revenue Exposure
30.50% of Monthly Revenue Is Tied to Churning Customers
Churn puts £139,131 in monthly revenue — £1.67M annualised — at risk. Nearly one-third of recurring revenue is associated with customers who ultimately leave.
05
Customer Value
The Business Is Losing Its Highest-Value Customers
Churned customers pay £74.44/month on average versus £61.27 for retained customers — roughly £13 more per month walking out the door with every churned account.
06
Contract Value
Two-Year Contracts Generate 2.7× More Revenue per Customer
Average revenue per customer reaches £3,728.93 on two-year contracts versus just £1,369.25 on month-to-month — despite month-to-month generating the highest total revenue through sheer customer volume.
07
Lifetime Value
Churned Two-Year Customers Carried 5× the LTV of Churned M2M Customers
Estimated LTV for churned two-year customers is £5,316.90, versus £1,023.51 for churned month-to-month customers — contract commitment is the strongest predictor of customer value retained.
08
Risk Pillar

Where the Business Is Structurally Exposed

Contract Concentration
Month-to-Month Customers Generate 56.41% of Monthly Revenue
Month-to-month customers make up 55.02% of the customer base but 56.41% of monthly revenue — meaning over half of recurring revenue depends on the segment most likely to churn.
09
Service Concentration
Fiber Optic Represents the Largest Single Concentration Risk
Fiber optic contributes 62.11% of monthly revenue while also recording the highest churn rate (41.89%) of any internet service — high dependency and high attrition in the same category.
10
Predictive Risk
814 High-Risk Customers Identified Before They Churn
Customers on month-to-month contracts, paying >£70/month, with <12 months tenure, churn at 69.53% — more than 3× the standard customer base rate (20.92%) — and can be flagged proactively.
11
Structural Flag

The business's two largest revenue sources — month-to-month contracts and fiber optic service — are also its two highest-churn segments. This concentration means retention initiatives targeted at these two groups carry disproportionate revenue protection value relative to broader, undifferentiated retention spend.

Business Recommendations

Where Retention Investment Should Go

01
Onboarding
Improve Early Customer Retention
With 47.44% of churn occurring within the first 12 months, structured onboarding programmes, first-year engagement campaigns, and early satisfaction surveys represent the highest-leverage intervention window in the customer lifecycle.
02
Contract Strategy
Encourage Long-Term Contract Adoption
Month-to-month churn (42.71%) is 15× that of two-year contracts. Discounted annual plans, contract upgrade incentives, and renewal promotions could shift customers toward materially lower-churn, higher-LTV agreements.
03
Service Quality
Investigate the Fiber Optic Customer Experience
Fiber optic generates 62.11% of revenue while recording the highest churn rate in the dataset. A targeted review of service quality, pricing competitiveness, and support interactions is warranted given the scale of revenue dependency.
04
Value Protection
Target High-Value Customers Before Churn
Churned customers pay £13 more per month on average than retained customers. Retention campaigns should prioritise premium, long-tenure, and rising-spend customers — the segments with the greatest revenue at stake per account.
05
Predictive Retention
Deploy Predictive Retention Programmes
The 814-customer high-risk profile (M2M · >£70/mo · <12mo tenure, 69.53% churn) demonstrates that at-risk customers can be identified using observable characteristics — enabling risk scoring, automated workflows, and early intervention ahead of churn.
